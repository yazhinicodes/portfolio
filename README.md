### 3️⃣ 🏪 Mobimart Planner: Capital-Constrained Stock Allocation Engine
[![Repo](https://img.shields.io/badge/View%20Repo-mobimart--planner-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/yazhinicodes/mobimart-planner)

A weekly stock allocation and end-of-life (EOL) risk engine for a **25-store mobile retail chain** operating under a hard **₹4 Crore working-capital cap**, built as a decision-support tool.

- 📈 Generates a realistic **12-month sales history** to work with
- 📅 Produces a **Monday allocation recommendation**, with the reasoning shown in rupees
- ⚠️ **Flags and prices out End-of-Life inventory risk**
- ⚖️ **Benchmarks itself honestly** against a naive proportional-allocation baseline, reporting **where it wins and where it genuinely loses**

**Stack:** `Python` `Streamlit`

> 💡 **Why it matters for this role:** it shows I can turn a messy real-world constraint into a usable interface, and that I report results honestly, including the unflattering ones.
